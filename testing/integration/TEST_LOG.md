# Integration — test log

Tests that two or more subsystems work together. There is one test (or more) for every interface listed in TEAM.md section 5. Both owners agree on the test and sign off the result.

See `testing/README.md` for how to run tests and record results.

## Test cases

Define a test here before running it. Test IDs never change.

| ID | What is tested | Requirement / interface | Method | Steps / how | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INT-01 | No junction, barcode or fault event is lost during a whole run | NFR11, IF-01 (all → S5) | manual | MQTT image; run the whole course for at least 5 min | In every `[telem] ... lost K/D` line, K = 0. D (dropped line samples) may be non-zero |
| INT-02 | Junction move: drive 130 mm, settle, pivot; each Motion reply starts the next step | FR5, IF-02, IF-03 (S5 ↔ S1) | manual | Map `A=left`; barcode A before a cross; START. This can be the first cross of the SYS-03 run, which uses the same mapping for A | The log shows, in order: `[sup] barcode 'A' stored -> LEFT ...`, `[line] cross at odo ...`, `[sup] junction: letter A -> LEFT (this barcode is now used up)`, `[sup] 3 -> 4`, `[sup] junction: stopped over the crossing, pivoting 90 deg`, `[sup] turn check: ...`, `[sup] 4 -> 3`. The car stops with its axle over the crossing (within 15 mm *(p)*) before it pivots |
| INT-03 | The line error steers the wheels the right way | FR1, IF-04 (S2 → S1) | manual | NORMAL image, on a stand, START. Hold black tape under the left sensor only, then the right only, then both | Left only: the right wheel runs faster than the left (the car would steer left). Right only: the mirror. Both: equal speeds |
| INT-04 | The scan mode follows the Supervisor state; GUARD pings every 50 ms | FR11, NFR2, IF-05 (S5 → S4) | manual | Scope on TRIG (GP4) | Idle: no TRIG pulses. LineFollow: a 10 µs pulse every 50 ms. During a width sweep or side probe: pulses only at each look |
| INT-05 | Supervisor events reach the PC with x/y | FR13, IF-06 (S5 → telemetry) | manual | `mosquitto_sub -t "mtk3smp-pico/event" -v` during a run that includes a barcode, a cross, the hump and an obstacle | One `{"ev":"state",...}` for every `[sup] A -> B` line, with matching names. `barcode`, `junction`, `command`, `hump` and `obstacle` events, each with x and y |
| INT-06 | Both halves of `nav_state` update | NFR9, IF-07 (S1, S3 → all) | auto | Check not written yet. `nav_read()` twice, 30 ms apart, with the IMU fitted | `m.t_us` and `i.t_us` have both changed |
| INT-07 | Every stop bit cuts the motors in the next cycle, and WIFI_DOWN does not | NFR3, FR16, IF-08 (all → all) | auto | Check not written yet. For each of the 10 bits in `FAULT_STOP_MASK`: clear all bits, send FOLLOW SLOW, wait 50 ms, raise the bit, wait 15 ms, check the duties, clear the bit. Then do the same with `FAULT_WIFI_DOWN` | Duties are 0 within 15 ms for all 10 stop bits. With WIFI_DOWN set the motors keep running |
| INT-08 | The calibration handshake | FR15, IF-09 (S5 → S2, S3) | manual | Calibrate as in S2-U07. Then calibrate again with the IMU unplugged | Both runs: `[line] calibrate OK`, the IMU reports done (straight away when there is no IMU), a 100 ms beep and `[sup] 2 -> 1`, about 16 s *(p)* after the start |
| INT-09 | Line search on the car finds the line again | FR2, IF-10 (S5 → S2) | manual | A track with a sharp corner that the car overshoots, or a 100 mm gap *(p)* in the line | `[sup] 3 -> 7`. The car pivots 20° toward the side where it last saw the line, then 40° the other way, back to the centre, then creeps 50 mm. `[sup] 7 -> 3` when it sees the line |
| INT-10 | Line search gives up after 300 mm and enters Fault | FR2, FR16 | manual | Start on a short line that ends in a large white area | Once the search has moved the car 300 mm net: `[sup] FAULT: LINE_SEARCH_FAIL (0x...)`, red LED, motors stopped |
| INT-11 | Each subsystem acts only in its own states | NFR9, IF-11 (`sys_state_get()`) | manual | In Idle, push the car by hand over a barcode, a cross and the hump | No `[bc] read`, no `[line] cross`, no `[imu] hump start`, and no state change |
| INT-12 | The hump slows the car to hump speed and it returns to cruise | FR9, FR7, IF-01 (S3 → S5) | manual | Drive over the practice hump | `[sup] 3 -> 5` at `hump start`. Telemetry speed level 2 and `v` about half the cruise value *(p)* (`speed_pm` 43 against 86). The car keeps following the line. `[sup] 5 -> 3` right after `hump end`, which needs 100 ms back at level. Event `{"ev":"hump","height_mm":N,...}` |
| INT-13 | Width stop once at 40 cm, then the bypass stop at 21 cm | FR11, IF-01 (S4 → S5), IF-05 | manual | A box on the line; the car follows the line toward it | One `[sup] obstacle ahead at N cm: stopping to measure its width` with N < 40, and no second one for the same box. The sweep takes about 5 to 6 s. `[sup] obstacle width W mm` and `[sup] bypass side ...`. The car drives on, then stops at the 21 cm reading (`[sup] 3 -> 6`) |
| INT-14 | Obstacle stop latency, from S4 through the fault flag to S1 | FR11, NFR3, IF-08 | manual | Scope on TRIG (GP4) and GP8 (left motor). Car following on a stand; bring a board quickly to 15 cm in front | The motors cut within 10 ms of the ping that decides the stop, and within 160 ms *(p)* of the board arriving (2 pings plus one motion cycle) |
| INT-15 | Remote START / STOP from the web panel | FR15 | manual | Web panel: START, wait 5 s, STOP | The car starts within 1 s *(p)* and stops within 1 s *(p)*. Console: `[sup] remote START`, `[sup] remote STOP`. The panel log shows both acks |
| INT-16 | A new barcode replaces the held letter | FR5 | manual | Map `A=left,B=right`. Barcode A, then barcode B, then a cross, then a second cross with no barcode before it | `[sup] barcode 'A' stored -> LEFT ...`, `[sup] barcode 'B' stored -> RIGHT ...`, `[sup] junction: letter B -> RIGHT (this barcode is now used up)`, and the car turns right. At the second cross: `[sup] junction: letter - -> straight on (no barcode: the default)` |
| INT-17 | Compass check and one correction after a junction pivot | FR8, NFR4 | manual | Test builds with a wrong turn scale, so the encoder pivot misses: (a) `TURN_TRACK_X10 1100` in `hw_alloc.h` (pivot about 82°, error about 8°); (b) `700` (about 52°, error about 38°). Calibrated compass, map `A=left`, barcode A, cross. (c) Build (a) again without calibrating the compass. Restore 1208 afterwards | (a) `[sup] turn check: compass moved .., wanted -90, error E` with E between 5 and 30 either way, one corrective pivot, and the final heading within ± 5° of the floor mark. (b) E over 30: logged, no correction. (c) the line ends in `(compass not calibrated: not used)`, no correction |
| INT-18 | Which obstacle checks run in each Running state | FR11 | manual | Scope on TRIG (GP4) while the car goes through LineFollow, Hump, a junction (drive and pivot), LineSearch after a lost line, and a bypass that ends in LineSearch. In each, put a box at 15 cm, then at 4 cm | Pulses every 50 ms in every phase except the bypass pivots and the LineSearch after the bypass. LineFollow, Hump and LineSearch after a lost line: the 15 cm box stops the car (`[sup] N -> 6`). Junction drive and pivot, bypass side-steps and approach: only the 4 cm box stops it. Passing the box: a box ahead does not stop it, and one close beside it gives `[sup] bypass: too close while passing`. Bypass pivots and LineSearch after the bypass: no pulses, no stop |
| INT-19 | One owner task per device; subsystems talk only through `TEAM.md` section 5 | NFR9 | manual | Grep `app_program/` for each driver's calls (`drv_motor_`, `drv_adc_`, `drv_imu_`, `drv_servo_`, `us_ping`, `drv_led_`, `drv_buzzer`) and for `IMPORT` of another subsystem's variables. Compare with `TEAM.md` sections 3 and 5 | Each driver is called only from its owner's files. The exceptions NFR9 allows: `diag_pm.c` `safe_outputs()` writes the motor and buzzer pins on a watch trip; `telem_snapshot()` reads `bc_stats`, `imu_peak_mm` and `sup_stats`; `telem_outbox` and the event ring carry data to the Wi-Fi task. Any other exception is a FAIL |

*Method:* **auto** = `TEST_CHECK` in code, result printed on the console;
**manual** = a person observes the result (LED, scope, meter, phone app…).
An **auto** row whose steps start "Laptop unit test" runs on a laptop with `make -C tests/host -f host_tests.mk CC=gcc`; one whose steps start "Check not written yet" needs its `TEST_CHECK` added first.
